<p>The early migrants are just starting to arrive in Toronto, but before I get too carried away with all the new species arriving in town, I thought I would close out the late-winter-early-spring season with some highlights. These are some of my favorite images from March and April. </p>

<p>As the spring migration picks up, this is an exciting time to go for a walk in the woods with a pair of binoculars, or even just sleep with your window open to hear the commotion of the newly arrived birds out your window in the morning. Either way, happy birding!</p>
