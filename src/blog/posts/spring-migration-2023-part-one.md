<p>The spring migration is on in Toronto! The past month has been exciting as the early migrants started showing up, and then in the past few days the trees have exploded with the arrival of the Warblers. As I write this we’re right in the middle of peak migration, so I’m calling this post “part one” because I expect to see a lot more in the next week. Here are my highlights from late April and early May:</p>
